Ask us a question ↗
How do I request a cleaning quote?

Complete the enquiry form with your contact details, preferred service, and a description of your space. The business can then discuss the scope and price with you.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

Supply arrangements vary by business. Ask whether cleaning products and equipment are included or need to be provided.

Do you clean offices as well as homes?

Office cleaning is listed as a sample service on this website. Confirm that the business offers it and whether it can work around the office's operating hours.

How is the final price determined?

Pricing may depend on property size, condition, requested tasks, frequency, location, and other job requirements. Request a quote before confirming the booking.

Can I reschedule my cleaning appointment?

Contact the business to confirm its actual rescheduling, cancellation, and deposit policies before booking.
